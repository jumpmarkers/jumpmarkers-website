Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

JumpMarkers ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that allows users to bookmark YouTube timestamps. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. JumpMarkers stores all your markers, folders, tags, and preferences exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

2. What Is Stored Locally

  • Your YouTube timestamp markers (title, time, video URL)
  • Folder and tag names you create
  • Extension preferences (theme, hotkeys, seek step, etc.)
  • Premium license key (stored locally to validate your purchase)

None of this information is shared with us or any third party.

3. Premium License

If you purchase a Premium license, the transaction is processed by Lemon Squeezy (our payment provider). We receive only a license key to activate your account. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Please refer to Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle payment data.

4. Analytics & Tracking

JumpMarkers does not use any analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind. We have no visibility into how you use the extension.

5. Permissions

JumpMarkers requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • storage — to save your markers, folders, tags, and preferences locally on your device
  • tabs / windows — to detect the currently open YouTube video and send commands to the page (seek, pause, add marker) when using global hotkeys from other browser tabs
  • host permission (youtube.com) — to inject the content script that adds timeline indicator dots on YouTube's progress bar and enables keyboard shortcuts within the player

None of these permissions are used to collect, read, or transmit personal data.

6. Children's Privacy

JumpMarkers is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at jumpmarkers@gmail.com.
