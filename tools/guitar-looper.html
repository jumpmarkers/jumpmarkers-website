This tool is free — and it always will be. If you've found it useful, or you simply enjoy seeing independent developers build things like this, the best way to support this work is to give JumpMarkers a try. It's a free Chrome extension built for people who learn music from YouTube — mark exact moments in a song, loop sections while you work out the notes, scrub frame by frame with audio to catch every detail, and organize everything into folders by song or technique.
