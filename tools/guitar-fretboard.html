🖱 Mouse
Click on dim noteHighlight the note across the whole fretboard
Click on highlighted noteUn-highlight it (keeps it visible)
Click on visible noteHide it from the fretboard
Ctrl + ClickSet / unset that note as Root
Alt + ClickHide / show that specific note at that exact fret & string
Shift + ScrollZoom in / out on fret width
⠿ Drag & Drop
Drag ⠿ handleReorder fretboards. A mini-map appears for quick navigation.
⬜ Box Select
Drag on fretboardDraw a selection rectangle — notes inside are highlighted in orange
DELHide all selected notes at those exact positions (same as Alt+click, one by one)
EscapeCancel the selection without hiding anything
Ctrl+ZUndo the hide after pressing DEL
