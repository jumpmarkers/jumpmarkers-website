Chrome Extension

Playlist Organizer

The YouTube playlist manager that should have been built into YouTube. Move videos, find duplicates, merge similar playlists and search across everything — right from your browser.

Add to Chrome — Free

What it does

🚚

Move Videos

Right-click any video and move it to another playlist instantly. Select multiple videos and move them all at once.

🔍

Search Everywhere

Search for any video title across all your playlists at once — no more opening each list one by one.

📺

Filter by Channel

Right-click a video and show only videos from that creator within the playlist. Great for large lists.

✏️

Manage Playlists

Rename, delete, and create playlists without leaving your current YouTube page.

🗂️

Find Duplicates

Scans all your playlists and surfaces every video that appears more than once, so you can clean up with one click.

🔀

Merge Playlists

Detects playlists with similar names and merges them automatically — videos moved, duplicate source deleted.
